Install Instructions
1. Download new display / Open saved display.
(Option download is depending on the author)
2. Tap Install button, Mi Fit app will be open. Make sure this app and your band already connected.
3. Tap Profile in bottom navigation.
4. Tap Mi Smart Band 4 row.
5. Tap Band display settings row.
6a. Tap third tab. If there are only two tab, go to 6b step.
6b. Tap first tab.
7. Select specified display and Sync.
